Privacy (NDPA)

What we collect

Phone number and OTP verification status, KYC documents you submit for review, gameplay/session data stored on this device (balance, history, preferences), and technical data such as IP address for security.

Your rights under the NDPA

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and can withdraw consent for optional processing at any time.

  • Access & export — request a copy of the data linked to your account.
  • Correction — fix inaccurate KYC or profile details.
  • Deletion — use "Reset all data" for on-device demo data, or request full account deletion.

Storage

Demo gameplay data lives only in this browser's local storage. Verified-account data (phone, KYC, staff review notes) is stored server-side with restricted staff access.
